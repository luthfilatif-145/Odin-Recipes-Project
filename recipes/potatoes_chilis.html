You can make a fast and easy Chili Cheese Baked Potato dinner in about 25 minutes using just a few basic ingredients.


Chili Cheese Baked Potato Recipe


  • Prep time: 5 minutes
  • Cook time: 20 minutes
  • Servings: 2

Ingredients


  • 2 medium russet potatoes (10 to 12 ounces each)
  • 1 teaspoon olive oil
  • Salt (to taste)
  • 1 can (15-ounce) chili
  • ½ to 1 cup grated cheddar cheese (2 to 4 ounces)
  • Optional toppings: Sour cream, green onions, or cilantro
