English Loaf Cake


Here is a simple and fast recipe for an English Loaf Cake. It is light, soft, and easy to bake in a standard bread tin.


Ingredients


  • 1 cup all-purpose flour
  • ½ cup white sugar
  • 1 teaspoon baking powder
  • 2 large eggs
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
  • ¼ cup vegetable oil
  • ½ cup milk

Instructions


  1. Mix the wet base: Beat the eggs and vanilla together in a bowl. Add the sugar and beat until the mix looks pale and light.
  2. Add liquids: Pour in the vegetable oil and stir. Then, pour in the milk and mix well.
  3. Add dry ingredients: Sift the flour and baking powder together. Gently fold them into the wet mix using a large spoon.
  4. Prepare the pan: Grease a rectangular loaf pan and dust it lightly with a little flour.
  5. Bake: Pour the batter into the pan. Bake at 150°C (302°F) on the middle oven rack until done (about 1 hour to 1 hour 15 minutes).
  6. Cool and serve: Let the cake cool completely on a wire rack before you slice and eat it.
