Saus Recipes


You can make flavorful homemade Italian sausage meat at home in just a few minutes using simple ground pork or beef and classic Italian spices.


Homemade Italian Sausage Ingredients


  • Ground meat: 500g ground pork or pork shoulder
  • Fennel seeds: 1 teaspoon (crushed, essential for authentic flavor)
  • Paprika: 1 teaspoon (sweet or regular)
  • Garlic powder: 1 teaspoon (or finely grated fresh garlic)
  • Onion powder: 1/2 teaspoon
  • Dried herbs: 1/2 teaspoon each of dried basil, oregano, and thyme
  • Salt & black pepper: 1 teaspoon salt and 1 teaspoon black pepper
  • Red chilli flakes: 1/2 teaspoon (optional, for a spicy kick)

Quick Instructions


  1. Mix the spices: In a small bowl, combine the fennel seeds, paprika, garlic powder, onion powder, basil, oregano, thyme, salt, pepper, and chilli flakes.
  2. Combine with meat: Place the ground meat in a large bowl. Sprinkle the spice mix evenly over the meat.
  3. Knead: Use your hands to gently mix the spices into the meat until well distributed. Do not overmix to keep a tender texture.
  4. Cook or store: Shape the mixture into patties, use it loose in pasta sauces, or stuff it into casings if making traditional links. Fry in a pan over medium heat with a little oil until browned and cooked through.
